How we work

The journey of your goods, from the moment the container arrives until it leaves.

01

Indirect User registration

If you’re a domestic importer and you choose to operate in the La Plata Free Zone (ZFLP), we handle your registration as an Indirect User. It’s a simple, one-time process that takes 48 to 72 hours. To get started:

1. Send the required documents.
2. Pay the corresponding annual fee.

Contracts run for up to 60 months.

*Foreign companies not registered in Argentina don’t need to complete this registration.

Before you operate
02

Cargo pickup and transfer

We handle the pickup of your air cargo, deconsolidated cargo or container from the port terminal and its transfer to the ZFLP. Containers are deconsolidated on arrival. We also coordinate the return of the empty container where required. Security escort during transport is optional.

Inbound · optional
03

Heavy machinery

A specialized technician with a valid operator license handles the in/out movement of heavy machinery, unloading your goods from the truck on arrival and loading them when you collect them.

Inbound and outbound
04

Palletizing and stretch wrapping

We place your goods on pallets and wrap them in clear film to make storage easier, prevent damage and improve stowage during distribution.

Inbound
05

Goods storage

When your goods arrive, we check items and quantities against the packing list and the operation’s documentation. Then we decide the best way to store them, in racks or on the floor, depending on the characteristics of your goods and what we agree with you.

We have an outdoor yard for storing heavy machinery.

We adapt to your cargo and to how you need to work with it.

While in storage
06

Stock control and reporting

From arrival, with a detailed declaration of products and quantities, through to departure, we monitor your goods thoroughly to prevent errors and give you accurate information whenever you need it. We also keep a photo record of your goods as backup.

Throughout the process
07

Order preparation

You can collect your goods in full or in part, according to your needs. That way you always have stock ready for immediate delivery.

Outbound
08

Tax stamping and labeling

On request, we apply tax stamps and labels to your units to meet tax obligations or to identify your products.

Outbound · on request
09

Scale and reshipment certificate

We weigh your goods at every entry and exit to confirm they match the customs documentation. When you take products out, we issue the reshipment certificate, which deducts them from your stock with the concessionaire.

Inbound and outbound
10

Freight and distribution

We handle the transport of your goods from the ZFLP to the destination you specify. Security escort during the trip is optional.

Outbound · optional

Frequently asked questions

How does a free zone work?

A free zone is an area inside the country but outside the general customs territory, where goods can enter, be stored and be processed without paying import duties or other taxes while they remain there. When goods leave, there are two paths. If they are re-exported, no import taxes apply. If they are destined for the local market, they are cleared through customs and taxes are paid only at that point. This way, importers don’t tie up capital before they know what they will do with their goods. Everything takes place in an area controlled by Customs and managed by an operator.

Can I work with my own customs broker?

Yes, you can work with the customs broker you trust. We coordinate the logistics with the professionals involved in your operation to make receiving, storing and releasing your goods smooth.

What types of goods can be stored?

We handle goods from many sectors, from industrial supplies and technology products to machinery and mining-related goods. Each shipment is assessed based on the characteristics of the cargo, its dimensions, its weight, and the handling and storage conditions it requires.

Are my goods insured?

Coverage depends on the terms you contract and the policy that applies to each operation. Before confirming the service, contact us to find out what coverage applies to your goods and what it includes.

Can you work on my goods inside the warehouse?

Yes. Depending on the characteristics of the cargo and the needs of each operation, we can arrange tasks such as palletizing, stretch wrapping, labeling and order preparation. We analyze each request to define the most convenient way of working.

Can I collect my goods in partial shipments?

Yes, you can bring in your goods and collect them in parts, in different stages, according to your operation’s needs. This lets you organize your dispatches better, prepare specific orders and schedule pickups around your own timelines and business requirements.

How can I check the status of my stored goods?

We monitor your goods from arrival to departure and give you stock information whenever you need it, with photo records as backup. You are also always welcome to visit our warehouses, by prior appointment.

How do I request a quote for my operation?

Contact us and we’ll advise you to put together the proposal that best fits your operation. To do that, we need to know the type of goods, the estimated volume, the origin and destination, and the planned storage time.
